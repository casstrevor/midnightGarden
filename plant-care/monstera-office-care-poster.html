MONSTERA DELICIOSA

Swiss Cheese Plant · Office Care Guide

Your cutting · currently rooting in water

01

Water or Soil?

Keep it in water while roots form, and change the water weekly. Transfer to soil when white roots are 2–4 inches long. Use a chunky aroid mix: potting soil + perlite + orchid bark. After potting, water when the top 1–2 inches of soil feel dry.

02

Attach to a Trellis

Monsteras are climbers. Put a moss pole or wood trellis in the pot, set the stem against it, and loosely secure with soft velcro plant ties or twine every few inches. Guide aerial roots toward the pole. Never cinch tight or force the leaves.

03

Grow Light & Schedule

Use a full-spectrum white LED grow light (~4000–6500K). Skip colored decorative LEDs—they look fun but are weak for growth. Run the light 12–14 hours daily, then 10–12 hours of dark. An outlet timer makes this easy.

04

Light Distance

Keep the grow light 12–24 inches from the leaves. Start farther away; move closer if growth looks leggy. If leaves feel hot or bleach pale, back the light up. Side lighting is fine—avoid blasting one spot all day.
